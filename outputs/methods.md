I–V curves were recorded with a PROVA 200A analyser (150 points per sweep, current-swept). Voltage outliers were removed with a Hampel filter (window 7, 3σ), and residual off-trend points with an iterative robust criterion (4σ, orthogonal residuals). Each curve was represented by a cubic B-spline (30 knots) constrained to be monotonically decreasing (and concave unless a bypass-diode step was present in both repeat sweeps; 14 of 53 valid sweeps). Near short circuit the spline was replaced by a robust straight line (the shunt segment) blended in over 0.05 Voc. Pmax was taken from the fitted curve and validated against a fourth-order polynomial fit of P(V) around the maximum power point (median deviation 0.26 % over the 46 sweeps whose data bracket the peak; 0.28 % over all 53). Rs and Rsh were obtained from the slopes of the fitted curve at Voc and Isc and from a single-diode model fit, reported only when the fit passed its quality gate (nRMSE ≤ 1.2 %, 0.9 ≤ n ≤ 2.5; 15 of 53 sweeps). 3 sweeps failed the validity check (incomplete current range or dark panel) and were excluded from the statistics.
